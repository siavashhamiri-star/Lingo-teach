# **App Name**: LinguaWeave

## Core Features:

- Bilingual Chatbot: An AI chatbot capable of communicating fluently in both Persian and English, leveraging film and speech snippets for conversational learning. The chatbot can also provide online examinations and scores.
- Personalized Learning Path: AI analyzes the user's knowledge through interviews and creates tailored lessons and exercises using reasoning as a tool.
- Roleplay: A system which leverages the AI chatbot to conduct dynamic conversational roleplays.
- Multilingual Support: Support for both Persian speakers learning English and English speakers learning Persian.
- Interactive Exercises: Varied exercises for grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation, incorporating animation, cartoons, photos, videos, and music.
- User Profile: Each user has a profile to store their language proficiency, learning preferences, and progress data.
- Online Community: A platform for users to interact via text, voice chat, and video calls (premium).
- Daily Language Challenges: Free users can participate in up to two daily challenges against up to 4 other users, answering questions from the chatbot via text or voice. Winners get stars. Users can spend stars to enter more challenges. Top performers each week advance to the Premier League, which takes place once a month and costs stars to enter.
- Language Exchange Rooms: Users can create and join language exchange rooms based on their proficiency level for text and voice conversations; being active in a room is required. The app can also link to HelloTalk and similar apps.
- Personal AI Tutor: Users can ask the AI chatbot questions, and translate phrases from Persian to English or English to Persian, 10 queries per day in free tier and 50 queries per day in premium.
- Spaced Repetition (Leitner Box): A system for spaced repetition learning.
- Bilingual Short Stories: Once a week, the chatbot will provide a short bilingual story, reading it aloud sentence by sentence in both languages.
- Adaptive Learning Levels: The app assesses learners and assigns a level from 1-160, where 1 is beginner and 160 is advanced, for both Persian to English and English to Persian proficiency.
- Weekly Listening Comprehension: Once a week, the chatbot will read an article, speech, or news segment in the target language, and the user simply listens.
- Weekly Translation Exercise: Each week, the chatbot provides a text in both Persian and English based on the user's language level and asks them to translate it.
- Premium Karaoke: Premium users get access to a weekly English song with the vocals removed, allowing them to sing karaoke and familiarize themselves with the language through singing. Includes echo, reverb, and saving capabilities.
- Referral Program: Each learner can introduce two new learners using a referral code and receive one month of free premium features, or gift this to their referral if they are already premium.
- LinkedIn Integration: The app can be linked to LinkedIn profiles.
- AI-Powered Accent Training: The AI will analyze the user's pronunciation and give real-time feedback, including visual aids to show mouth and tongue placement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5) for trustworthiness and intellect.
- Background color: Light blue (#E3F2FD) provides a calm learning environment.
- Accent color: Warm orange (#FF9800) to highlight interactive elements and calls to action.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' sans-serif font provides a modern look combined with some warmth; the headline font will also be 'PT Sans'.
- Clean, universal icons representing language concepts, lessons, and exercises.
- Clean, intuitive layout with clear content hierarchy to make the app easy to navigate.
- Simple and user-friendly interface design.
- Subtle transitions and feedback animations to provide a smooth user experience.
